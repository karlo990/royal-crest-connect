For Royal Crest staff

Royal Crest Connect

Answer parents on WhatsApp, send notices and polls to every class group, and issue fee invoices and receipts as PDFs, all from your phone.

Download for Android Version 1.0 · 2.6 MB · Android 7 or newer

What it does

Parents' chats

The school's WhatsApp, on every staff phone. Reply, send photos, and see when a message was delivered.

Broadcasts

One notice to parents, class groups or staff. Nobody gets the same message twice.

Polls

Parents reply with a number and the app counts the votes for you.

Fees

Invoices and receipts on the school letterhead, sent to the parent as a PDF. Balances are tracked per pupil.

Install it

  1. Download the app on your Android phone with the button above.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps (Settings → Install unknown apps), then tap Install. It's signed by Royal Crest Academy.
  3. Open Royal Crest Connect and type the server address and access key. Ask the school office for them; they're never shared publicly.
  4. Type your name and tap Connect. Your name shows on the replies you send, so colleagues know who answered.

For the school office

The app talks to the school's own server, which keeps the chats, polls and fee records. A PC in the office links it to the school WhatsApp number and makes the PDFs.

The bursar can also use everything from a computer: open the server address in a browser and sign in.

Setup guide for the server and the office PC

Good to know

  • For staff only. Without the access key the app shows nothing.
  • Messages go out from the school's WhatsApp number, a few seconds apart.
  • Fee amounts are typed by the bursar; nothing is filled in until the fee schedule is set.
