How it works

Parents use WhatsApp. Staff use Connect.

Nothing changes for parents. The school's own server keeps the chats and fee records, and a PC in the office keeps the school WhatsApp number linked.

  1. Staff phones

    Reply, broadcast, poll and invoice from Royal Crest Connect.

  2. School server

    Keeps every chat, poll, invoice and receipt, behind an access key.

  3. Office PC

    Links the school WhatsApp number and prints the fee PDFs.

  4. Parents

    Get answers, notices, polls and PDFs on WhatsApp, as usual.

Staff onlyWithout the school's access key, the app shows nothing at all.

Questions

Good to know.

Do parents need to install anything?

No. Parents keep using WhatsApp exactly as they do now. Messages, notices, polls and PDFs come from the school's usual WhatsApp number.

Does it work on iPhone?

The app is for Android phones. On an iPhone, a tablet or a computer, open the school server's address in the browser and sign in to the staff dashboard: it has the same chats, broadcasts, polls and fees.

Why does my phone warn me when I install it?

Android shows that warning for every app that doesn't come from the Play Store. Royal Crest Connect is signed by Royal Crest Academy. You can compare the file's fingerprint with the one in the download box above.

Who can see the chats and fee records?

Only staff who have the school's access key. Fee PDFs live at private links that also need the key. If a phone is lost, the office changes the key and the old phone can't open anything.

Will parents get the same message twice?

No. The same text never goes to the same chat twice within 24 hours, even if someone taps Send again.

Where are the fee amounts set?

The bursar types them on each invoice. Once the fee schedule is approved, the office can save the standard amounts so they fill in by themselves.
